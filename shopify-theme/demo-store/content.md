## Pages

### About
Ferra Studio makes a small number of things in natural materials: linen, wool, waxed canvas, leather and clay. We work with five workshops in Portugal, Italy and Spain, and we visit each of them every season.

Nothing is produced for a sale rack. When a piece sells out it may come back next season, usually a little better than before.

### Shipping
Orders ship within two business days in recycled packaging. Standard delivery takes three to five business days. Free shipping on orders over $150.

### Returns
You have 30 days to return unworn pieces with their tags. Start a return from your account or write to us.

### Care guide
**Linen**: wash cool, line dry, wear it wrinkled. **Wool**: air it out between wears, hand wash rarely, dry flat. **Waxed canvas**: brush off dirt, re-wax once a year. **Leather**: a drop of balm each season.

## Journal (blog handle: `journal`)

### Notes from the linen mill
Our linen is woven on shuttle looms in northern Portugal, then washed twice before it reaches the cutting table. The second wash is what gives it that worn-in hand from the first day. Tags: Materials, Makers.

### How we choose a workshop
We look for three things: people who would rather make fewer pieces well, a place we can visit, and a price we can pay without asking anyone to cut corners. Tags: Makers.

### A field guide to waxed canvas
Waxed canvas is cotton duck soaked in paraffin and beeswax. It creases where you use it most, and those creases are the point. Tags: Materials, Care.
