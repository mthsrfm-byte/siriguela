# Theme Store listing (draft)

**Name:** Vereda (provisional) · **Presets:** Vereda, Murta · **Price hypothesis:** US$ 320 (range allowed: $100–$500 in $10 steps; to validate against comparable editorial themes before submission)

**Tagline (52/70):** An editorial theme for brands with something to say.

## Description
Vereda is built for independent brands that sell considered products and want their store to read like a magazine without losing what makes it convert. Oversized type, numbered indexes and a quiet palette put your photography first, while native filtering, a fast cart drawer and accessible components keep shopping simple.

Change the whole identity from the editor: five color schemes, three font roles, a scale for headings and body, corner radius, spacing and a signature accent mark you can turn into a dot, a square or nothing at all.

## Key highlights (three images, 1600 × 1200)
1. **Editorial composition** — a hero with separate desktop and mobile media, an editorial index and a lookbook mosaic that turn product stories into pages people scroll.
2. **Cover product pages** — a large first image, a confident title and a buy panel built from blocks, with swatches, gift card recipients, pickup and app blocks.
3. **Shop without friction** — native filters, predictive search, quick add on cards and a cart drawer that updates in place.
