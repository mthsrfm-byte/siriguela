# Theme name

**Provisional recommendation: Vereda** (Portuguese for "footpath"). Not final: requires trademark clearance and your approval. Shopify does not allow renaming after upload.

Rules applied (Shopify Theme Store): 1–2 word noun, under 30 characters, no collision with existing themes/presets, Shopify products or industries; one preset must carry the theme name.

Method (21 Nov 2026): `themes.shopify.com/themes/<name>` for each candidate (all returned 404; `/themes/dawn` returned 302 as control), plus a crawl of the full preset catalogue (356 themes, 1,377 presets) for exact and near matches, and a web search on ThemeForest, WordPress, Squarespace, Webflow and Framer. **This is a collision scan, not legal clearance.** Check USPTO, EUIPO and INPI before committing.

| # | Name | Meaning | English legibility | Collisions found | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vereda** | footpath | Easy: ve-RAY-da | None exact. Nearest: "Verdant" preset (Align theme) | **Recommended**. Evokes a considered journey, fits storytelling |
| 2 | **Murta** | myrtle | Easy | None | Used as the second preset (beauty/home) |
| 3 | Ondina | undine / water spirit | Medium | None | Good for beauty; softer |
| 4 | Quadra | city block / court | Easy | WordPress "Quadra" (Automattic), Framer template | Distinctive on Shopify, weaker overall |
| 5 | Sabia | thrush (Sabiá) | Medium; loses accent | None | Charming but accent-dependent |

Rejected: **Oiti** (your working name: clean, but hard to pronounce for English speakers: "oy-CHEE"), **Folio** (preset exists in Borders), **Atelie** (Atelier theme exists), **Lume** (ThemeForest Shopify fashion theme with same name; crowded "Lum-" field), **Aurea** (crowded "Aur-" field), **Tessa** (a first name, not a noun), **Cajá** (accent; "caja" = box in Spanish).

Domains were not purchased or checked for availability with a registrar.
